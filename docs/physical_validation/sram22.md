Preserve revision, corner, pins, configuration, derived-view procedure, and patches. Wrapper routing supports its recorded integration path; it does not establish independent signoff, macro-internal E5 energy, a physical fault map, radiation qualification, or lifecycle inventory.

The common max-transition bypass and residual DRVs remain visible. Never edit a Liberty constraint to convert failure into a passing claim.
