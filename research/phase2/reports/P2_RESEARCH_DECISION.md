They establish useful reproducibility and readiness work, but not a new ECC energy mechanism.

Any contribution remains conditional on actual estimator divergence, explainable physical effects or workload sensitivity. No predictive model, new algorithm, technology scaling, SRAM integration or Phase III campaign was started.
